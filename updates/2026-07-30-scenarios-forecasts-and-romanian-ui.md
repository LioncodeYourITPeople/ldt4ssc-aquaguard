Two services the municipalities asked for are usable in the dashboard.

Civil-protection staff can read flood risk on the map. The what-if service lets them draw a retention or drainage change and see a heuristic spatial effect. ECMWF forecasts, via Open-Meteo, cover the municipal bounding boxes as a grid. Data sharing between the platform’s catalogue and the rest of the stack is switched on.

The interface and the user manuals are available in Romanian as well as English and Greek, so Limanu staff can use the same screens as Pangaion.
