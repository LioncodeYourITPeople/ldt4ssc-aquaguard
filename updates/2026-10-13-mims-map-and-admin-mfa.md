The October gap-closing pass made the flood picture clearer for staff and tighter for personal data.

A MIMs Plus working log records MIM0 (accessing data) and MIM2 (representing data) against the Living-in.EU checklist, with the NGSI-LD flood models as the payloads. The pipeline publishes a 20 m soil-saturation map. Flood-risk views include uncertainty. Administrators can turn on a second factor in Keycloak. Citizen phone numbers and email addresses stay out of operational logs.

The MIMs note is a living tracker in the code repository. It covers the requirements reviewed so far.
