The citizen side of early warning is open in the platform.

A citizen role can register an email address and a phone number for alerts. Staff still approve a send before it goes out. Email delivery uses Mailtrap in the current environment, with seed rules so the path can be tested. One-time passwords are set up on first login when that option is on, and a person can delete their account.

Public self-signup is switched off. Municipalities enrol the people who should receive alerts.
