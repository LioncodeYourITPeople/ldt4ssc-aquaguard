AquaGuard began as a fork of another local digital twin. Renaming the repository was the small part. The pilot only became AquaGuard after the old place names, the air-quality models, and the leftover filters were removed from the dashboard, the broker, the tests, and the documentation.

## What we are taking forward

When a pilot reuses an existing twin, the cutover is a checklist: names, locales, data models, sample content, and tests all have to describe the new municipalities and the new domain. Leaving any of those behind makes later features look finished while they still speak the previous project.

We cleared that list before treating the Sentinel pipeline and the flood-risk models as the baseline.
