# AquaGuard platform

Sensor-free local digital twin for flood resilience in Pangaion (Greece) and Limanu (Romania).

## Purpose

Give a small or medium municipality early flood-risk information and a way to test retention or drainage changes, using Copernicus Sentinel imagery, ECMWF forecasts, and open geospatial layers.

## What it contains

Ingestion pipelines, NGSI-LD publishing, a soil-saturation CNN, a LightGBM flood-risk classifier, what-if scenarios, a staff dashboard, and citizen alerts that a person approves before they are sent. The main code areas are listed in [`src/README.md`](../src/README.md).
